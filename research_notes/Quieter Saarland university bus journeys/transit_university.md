# Saarbrücken bus routes and university timetable context

## Which bus trips are verified for the home and campus corridors?

### Takeaway
The current operator-linked timetable PDFs are valid **from 1 July 2026**. Exact weekday trips have been extracted for 101, 109, 112 and 124, preserving empty cells, short runs, direction, home stop and campus stop. The most useful auditable input is `bus_trips.csv`; this is scheduled service, not an occupancy measurement. **The user subsequently confirmed WS2026/27 and HTW/SWS as the home stop, with no travel-hour constraint. Filter pairs to home_stop = HTW / SWS for the primary heatmap/ranking.**

### Cited Findings
- The operator's current timetable index links separate direction tables and identifies separate 112 lecture-day and non-lecture-day schedules. The current four flyers explicitly print “gültig ab 1. Juli 2026”; the corresponding direction tables match their times. [Operator timetable index](https://www.saarbahn.de/fahrplan/fahrplaene); flyers: [101](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26a0ac19e0f), [109](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26a0b2e2a97), [111/112](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26a0b46d1fd), [124](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26a0b7eeb86).
- **124 to campus:** weekday daytime HTW/SWS departures 08:22, 08:52, 09:22, 09:52, and every 30 minutes onward through 18:52 reach Universität Campus 21 minutes later (:43 or :13) and Busterminal 23 minutes later (:45 or :15). The morning service has exceptional trips; the CSV includes the actual table rather than extrapolating the regular pattern. [124 toward university](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664516933a).

HTW/SWS is 24 minutes after terminal departure / 21 minutes after Campus departure, e.g. terminal16:17 → Campus16:20 → HTW16:41. The only school-day-marked columns on the first page are early Hbf→depot short runs without a campus time, so none affects the campus journey pairs. [124 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26645116974).
- **109 to campus:** Deutschherrnstraße07:22/07:52/08:22/08:52/09:22; then hourly10:22 through14:22; then half-hourly14:52 through18:52. These reach Universität Campus 28 minutes later, and terminal G31 minutes later. HTW/SWS is two minutes after Deutschherrnstraße. Some 109 buses start at Rathaus and cannot be boarded near home; the extraction excludes them from home→campus pairs. [109 toward university](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644b33c3a).
- **109 from campus:** terminal G :02/:26, Campus :05/:29, from07:02 through19:26 terminal /19:29 Campus. However **not every :29 bus goes to the home area**:07:29 continues to Deutschherrnstraße08:02;08:29–12:29 trips terminate at Rathaus;13:29–18:29 continue to HTW/SWS and Deutschherrnstraße;19:29 terminates at Rathaus. [109 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644b8fc21).

[112 lecture-day toward university](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644c9f73e).
- **112 lecture-day from campus:** terminal F → Mensa+1min → Campus+3min. Campus14:00,14:30,14:40,15:00,15:10,15:30,15:40,16:00,16:10,16:30,16:40,17:00,17:10,17:35,18:00,18:10 continue through to HTW/SWS21minutes later. [112 lecture-day toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644d6cf50).
- **101 to campus:** daytime HTW/SWS06:32, then :02/:32 through20:02, reach Campus21minutes later(:23/:53); later-night departures differ. Terminal C is two minutes after Campus; these buses continue toward Dudweiler. [101 toward Dudweiler](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664471a35c).
- **101 from campus:** weekday Campus06:26 then :56/:26 through19:56, with later exceptions, generally terminal A three minutes before Campus, arriving Betriebshof22minutes after Campus in the daytime. Example Campus16:26→Betriebshof16:48. The table does not print an HTW/SWS time in this direction; do not fabricate one. The 101 full stop diagram lists HTW/SWS and Roonstraße, but an exact omitted-stop time must come from the date-specific planner. `bus_trips.csv` therefore supplies verified Betriebshof alternatives, keeping HTW/SWS inbound absent. - The university identifies Campus for A/B/C, Mensa for C/D/E and DFKI/INM/MPI, Busterminal for HIPS/IZFP/KIST, and Stuhlsatzenhaus for CISPA. This means stop preference depends on the user's actual building. [UdS campus access](https://www.uni-saarland.de/standort/saarbruecken/bahn-bus.html).
- The operator currently states that normal bus line service is unaffected by the GDL strike; the substitute service concerns the S1. Thus the S1 strike should not be cited as cancelling these four bus routes. [Operator replacement-service notice](https://www.saarbahn.de/fahrplan/schienenersatzverkehr).

### Inferences
- Morning boarding at a home-area stop before Hauptbahnhof/Rathaus may secure a seat before the main city-centre student pickups. This is a route-order inference; no actual seat probabilities are available.
- Homebound boarding at terminal F/G/H on112/109/124 puts the user before the main Campus pickup3minutes later. The user may gain a seat by walking to the terminal and joining the queue early, especially if already near E buildings. It does not guarantee fewer students: the terminal itself can have a substantial queue. The101 has through passengers from Dudweiler.
- With c.t. classes releasing at13:45/15:45/17:45, the124 Campus :50 is just five minutes later and may intercept a release wave; choosing :20 of the next hour can avoid that particular wave, but exact-hour-ending classes create a competing wave. Actual course-ending sensitivity must decide between them.
- The analyst should rank **campus arrival time** for home→university journeys; bus departure at home :52 is not inherently quiet because the124 reaches Campus :13 immediately before common :15 starts.
- Use trip_id to deduplicate route supply when multiple alternative home stops are in `bus_trips.csv`. Stops on the same vehicle are not independent departure options.

### Gaps
- No route-specific measured passenger counts, onboard occupancy feeds, bus size assignments, boarding counts or student attendance data were found. Course counts cannot establish an empty bus or a numerical probability of getting a seat.
- Date-specific delays, cancellations and future timetable changes remain live-planner matters. The July2026 published timetable has no printed end date; it is the current linked schedule checked26October2026, not a guarantee that it remains unchanged throughout winter.
- The user confirmed HTW/SWS as their home stop. Campus stop remains to be chosen according to the actual building. Alternate home stops in the CSV are Deutschherrnstraße for109 and Betriebshof for101; these are optional and should not displace HTW in the requested main ranking. Walking times are estimates described below, not measured journey-planner paths.
- Tables list selected stops only. Missing intermediate times are not evidence that a bus skips a stop; no interpolation has been passed off as a verified departure.
- Extracted rows use clock times after midnight as printed; analyse daytime08:00–19:30 to avoid service-day ambiguity. Friday-only late trips are tagged. The non-lecture-day112 PDFs were downloaded and archived but intentionally excluded from the term-time paired dataset.

## Which home stops are plausible and how long is the access walk?

### Takeaway
The closest mapped stop is Deutschhausweg, while Deutschherrnstraße is a nearby109 stop with explicit printed timetable times. HTW/SWS and Betriebshof are also walkable alternatives, giving access to112/124/101. Do not confuse street-wide map summaries with the exact house location.

### Cited Findings
- The housing operator identifies the Moltkestraße3–5/7 Study Cubes as a short walk from htw and says buses immediately outside provide direct university service on lecture days. [Housing operator](https://www.sib-saarbruecken.de/mieten/studentenwohnen/studycubes).
- Primary OpenStreetMap geodata identifies the building at Moltkestraße5 near49.2312931,6.9743083; nearby Deutschhausweg stops near49.2315326,6.9737185 and49.2316518,6.9732613; Deutschherrnstraße stops near49.2321908,6.9755520 /49.2321016,6.9757852; HTW/SWS near49.2339516,6.9780260 /49.2337707,6.9779036; Betriebshof platforms near49.2336–49.23376,6.9712–6.97385. Raw Nominatim responses saved in `mapping_queries.json`, `mapping_stops_queries.json`, `mapping_depot_stops.json`. [Building](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/87261875); [Deutschhausweg](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/930054304); [Deutschherrnstraße](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/930011817); [HTW/SWS](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/5300080865); [Betriebshof](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/818631779).
- The current109 full stop list includes Deutschhausweg, Deutschherrnstraße and HTW/SWS before the city centre and then the university. Only Deutschherrnstraße and HTW/SWS are printed in its direction time table. [109 flyer with full stop list](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26a0b2e2a97).

### Inferences
- Approximate straight-line distances from the mapped house are50–90m to Deutschhausweg,135–140m to Deutschherrnstraße,380–400m to HTW/SWS and260–350m to Betriebshof platforms. Walking paths, crossings and platform choice make actual access longer.
- Reasonable provisional buffers are2–4minutes to Deutschherrnstraße,7–10minutes to HTW/SWS and5–8minutes to Betriebshof, plus a2–3minute arrival buffer at the stop. These are intentionally rounded estimates, not verified walking itineraries.
- Because Deutschhausweg times are not printed, using the explicitly timed Deutschherrnstraße stop adds a small walk while avoiding invented minute-level precision. The nearest stop should be checked in Saarfahrplan for the actual date if the user prefers it.

### Gaps
- The exact entrances, preferred pedestrian route, accessibility needs and willingness to walk to the terminal are unknown. Do not treat a straight-line distance as a validated walking route.
- Overpass access returned406 and did not yield additional route geometry. Nominatim queries succeeded; their raw responses are preserved. Public map page fetches were not consistently available to the web tool.

## What broader university timing evidence is available?

### Takeaway
Winter2026/27 is the actual current term and has published course listings. Computer-science schedules can be analysed, but this transit branch could not download the entire university LSF tree. Three engineering lecture rows form a tiny, explicitly labelled context sample and must not be presented as a whole-campus census.

### Cited Findings
- Wintersemester2026/27 runs1October2026–31March2027, lectures12October2026–5February2027, with teaching break21December2026–1January2027. [University semester dates](https://www.uni-saarland.de/dezernat/ls/termine.html); [official academic-year PDF](https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/dezernat/ls/semestertermine/Studienjahr2026-2027.pdf).
- The university's own welcome guide explains that a nominal10–12 class usually means10:15–11:45; c.t. is fifteen minutes later and s.t. begins punctually. It therefore supports sensitivity checks for quarter-hour start and end offsets; it does not justify overriding exact course-specific times. [Official welcome guide, class hours](https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/global/welcome-center/nadu.pdf).
- The current official LSF indexed sport-science branch is explicitly WS2026/27. It contains independent non-CS schedules and shows why a CS-only course count is incomplete. [Official LSF branch](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C455542%7C460146&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total).
- The current LMT teaching page publishes Microsensors Mondays14–16 in A5.1, Mikrosystemtechnik Thursdays10–12 in A5.1 room0.09 from22October2026, and Medizinische Messtechnik Thursdays10:15–11:45 in A5.1 room0.09 from22October2026. **The two Thursday courses have a potential room/time conflict on the source itself**; retain uncertainty rather than treating these as independent known groups. The Microsensors entry appears under both bachelor and master and has been deduplicated. [LMT current teaching](https://www.lmt.uni-saarland.de/index.php/en/current-news/aktuelles-lehre).

### Inferences
- Course ends at quarter-to even hours can generate the user's observed crowds at14:00/16:00/18:00 after walking and waiting. Other classes end exactly at those hours; a mixed-time model is needed.
- Entire faculties, school passengers, workers, canteen/library users and CS tutorials with unpublished schedules are omitted from a public course census. Even many observed class rows are not proportional to student numbers: a small seminar and a large lecture are not equal demand.
- Do not apply enrolment weights without actual sourced counts. Physical location helps distinguish campus release waves from online courses or Homburg activity.

### Gaps
- Both `https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=wtree&search=1&trex=total&root120262=461590&P.vx=lang` and the non-www equivalent timed out in raw HTTP after60/30seconds; the web tool also could not open the whole-tree variant. Official indexed subtrees and individual courses can still be accessible. This is a retrieval failure, not proof that the catalog is private or empty.
- No systematic non-CS census was completed in this transit branch. `broader_meetings.csv` contains only3unique engineering rows, with low-confidence flags on conflicting Thursday entries. There are0Homburg/online courses in that small sample and0enrolment counts.
- The user confirmed WS2026/27 before these notes were finalized.
