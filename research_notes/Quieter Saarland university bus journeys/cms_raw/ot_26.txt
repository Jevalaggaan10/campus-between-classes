Dear students,
Exercise sheet 1 is now available on CMS. The first tutorial will be held on Monday, October 26.
Contents
In this course we will cover basic concepts of optimal transport, such as
Transport maps and couplings
Rockafellar's theorem
Kantorovich duality
Brenier's theorem
If time permits, we will also cover
Wasserstein distances
Applications
Recommended prerequisites
A solid background in mathematics and a good knowledge of measure-theoretic probability theory at the level of
Stochastics I
. Knowledge of functional analysis will be advantageous, but not required, as necessary concepts and theorems will be recalled.
Lecture
Wednesday, 14:15-15:45, in SR6 (room 2.17) in building E2 4
Tutorial
Monday, 10:15-11:45, in SR6 (room 2.17) in building E2 4
The tutorial will take place approximately every second week. The exact dates will be announced.
Exam
Only students of Mathematics, Mathematics and Computer Science, and Actuarial and Financial Mathematics can take the exam.
