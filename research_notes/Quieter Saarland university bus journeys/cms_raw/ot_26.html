    Exercise Sheet 1

    Geschrieben am 22.10.26 von Felix Sachse

    Dear students,

    Exercise sheet 1 is now available on CMS. The first tutorial will be held on Monday, October 26.

    Contents

    In this course we will cover basic concepts of optimal transport, such as

    • Transport maps and couplings
    • Rockafellar's theorem
    • Kantorovich duality
    • Brenier's theorem

    If time permits, we will also cover

    • Wasserstein distances
    • Applications

     

    Recommended prerequisites

    A solid background in mathematics and a good knowledge of measure-theoretic probability theory at the level of Stochastics I. Knowledge of functional analysis will be advantageous, but not required, as necessary concepts and theorems will be recalled.
