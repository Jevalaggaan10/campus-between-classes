    Content:

    We will cover the following topics:

    • Groups: normal subgroups, group actions, Sylow's theorems, solvable groups
    • Rings: ideals, principal ideal rings, unique factorization domains, Chinese remainder theorem
    • Modules und algebras
    • Fields: finite fields, field extensions, algebraic closure, Galois theory
    • eventually: constructible numbers

     

    Exercises:

    There will be weekly exercise sheets which can be submitted each week per CMS. Submissions in fixed groups of 3 people are permitted.

    There will also be weekly tutorials each Monday from 8:30 to 10:00 in SR 9 where you can ask questions regarding the exercise and get an additional tutorial exercise sheet.

    On each exercise sheet you can attain up to 30 points, for attending the tutorial you get 5P each week and up to 10P for successful participation of the tutorial.

    You will need in average 25P/45P per week of the attainable points to be permitted for the exam.

     

    Prerequisites:

    The lecture follows directly Linear Algebra I and II in terms of content.
