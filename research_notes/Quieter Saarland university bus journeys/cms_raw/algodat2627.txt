Recently, a considerable number of students from non-teacher-education programs have registered for this course. Therefore, I would like to clarify the intended target audience.
This course is intended exclusively for students in
teacher education programs
, especially those preparing to teach at the
Sekundarstufe I
level. The course is
not creditable for students enrolled in other degree programs
and is conducted
entirely in German
.
If you are not studying in a teacher education program but are nevertheless interested in the course, please contact me before registering. I would be happy to discuss whether the course is likely to be beneficial to your study goals.
Please also note that there are other courses with similar titles. Before registering, please make sure that this is indeed the course you intend to take. Otherwise, you may miss important information, announcements, or deadlines related to the courses that are actually relevant to your studies.
Thank you for your interest and understanding.

Algorithmen und Datenstrukturen für Informatik-Lehramt Sekundarstufe I
Auszug aus dem Modulhandbuch Lehramt für die Sekundarstufe I im Fach Informatik
Lernziele / Kompetenzen
Die Studierenden lernen wichtige effiziente Datenstrukturen und Algorithmen kennen und implementieren diese. Die Studierenden lernen die grundlegenden Methoden des Entwurfs und der Analyse von Algorithmen und Datenstrukturen kennen und wenden diese an.
Inhalt
Abstrakte Datentypen und ihre Realisierung durch Datenstrukturen (u.a. Liste, Bäume, Graphen)
Sortier- und Suchverfahren
Graphalgorithmen
Rekursion
Teile- und Herrsche-Prinzip
Dynamische Programmierung
Untersuchung der Zeit- und Platzkomplexität von Algorithmen
Datenschutz
