Contents
This course follows on from
Stochastics I
and covers the following topics:
Conditioning on σ-algebras (conditional expectations, conditional distributions)
Basic concepts of stochastic processes
Kolmogorov’s extension theorem
Poisson processes
Gaussian processes
Brownian motion
Markov processes
Martingales
Recommended prerequisites
A solid background in mathematics and a good knowledge of measure-theoretic probability theory at the level of
Stochastics I
.
