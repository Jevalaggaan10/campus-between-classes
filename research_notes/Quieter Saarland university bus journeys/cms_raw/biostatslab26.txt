We have put slides for a brief bash/conda/python tutorial online that explains how to install and use the conda package manager to create isolated environments (collections of packages) for running Python for the BioStatsLab course. There is also a file "environment.yml", which specifies the packages you want to install. "conda env create" shoud do the job when the file is present.
2. Lecture slides from today (administrative business; Lecture 01-01) are online, and also the first exercise sheet (officially released tomorrow, but why not have an extra day).
Exam Preparation
As said in the lecture, the course is fast-paced, so please stay on top, do spend (much!) time on the exercises, and prioritize this one over advanced courses that might be better taken in later semesters. To help you, we will run a mock exam on the Friday before the Christmas holidays during the normal lecture time. You may or may not take it, pass or fail, there will be a grade on it, but it will not be recorded, it is just for your own information on how you are doing with the material.
As also said today, there are no prerequisites for the exam, but I can almost guarantee that if you do not work continuously on the material and exercises, the probability to fail the exam is 90%. This are empirical values from past semesters. Phrased positively: If you keep up with the material and exercises every week (and know how to solve them by yourself without LLM help), your chances of passing are very high.

We are aware that there is a time conflict with Single Cell Bioinformatics. However, the times of BioStatsLab are not flexible; they are synchronized with MInf 3, and these are fixed times for the last 10+ years and they will stay fixed until the Bachelor structure of all CS programs changes (maybe in the far future). So please be aware of two things.
1. The proper 3rd math course to take in the BSc Bioinformatics program is BioStatsLab, not Mathematics for Informatics 3! It should be taken in parallel to Bioinformatics 1 in the 3rd semester. In the 3rd semester, Single Cell Bioinformatics should not be taken. Leave it to later.
2. For Master students who take BioStatsLab, it makes absolutely sense to focus on BioStatsLab first and take advanced courses like Single Cell Bioinformatics in a later year, once you have the statistical foundations.
So in reality, these two lectures are not in conflict. If you want to argue for different scheduling though, for whatever reason, ask Single Cell Bioinformatics to be at a different time. Thank you for your understanding.
BioStatsLab (a BSc Bioinformatics Replacement for MInf3)
Basic mandatory course, B.Sc. Bioinformatics, Saarland University.
This course is not available for credit points to students of other programs! You are welcome to audit, but will not get ECTS.
Prerequisites
Mathematics (MInf1+Minf2, especially some  analysis and linear algebra); solid programming skills
Credits
9 ECTS credits
Required time
4V+2Ü (4 hours of lectures, 2 hours of tutorials per week)
Language
English
(although this is a basic course, it also serves as an additional prerequisite for international Master students,
and is hence given in English!)

but failure is highly likely if you do not participate actively (so come to the lectures, tutorials, work on the problems!)
Target audience
This course is offered as a
basic lecture
in the B.Sc.
Bioinformatics
program as a replacement for Mathematics for Informaticians 3 (MInf3).
Thus it should be taken in the 3rd semester, after completing MInf1 and MInf2, as well as Programming 1 & 2.
It should be taken in parallel to Bioinformatics 1 during the B.Sc. Bioinformatics program.
You will need some programming skills to qualify for the exam. Best would be Python, but you can use a language of your choice.
Please do not waste your time by attempting this course without a solid basis in programming.

tests for differential gene expression
