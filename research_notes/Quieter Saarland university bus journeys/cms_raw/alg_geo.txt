Algebraic Geometry
Lecturer:
Prof. Dr. Vladimir Lazić
Office hours: by appointment via email.
Assistant:
Dr. Massimiliano Alessandro
Office hours: by appointment via email.
The course will be held in English.
If a lecture takes place online, then the recorded Video-Lecture will be uploaded to this webpage. Each video will be available online for two weeks and then deleted.
Topics
The content includes:
sheaves and schemes
morphisms between sheaves and schemes
abstract varieties
properness and separatedness
projective morphisms, basepoint free divisors, ample divisors, blowups
sheaves of modules, invertible sheaves and divisors
differentials and canonical sheaves, smoothness revisited
cohomology of sheaves, Čech cohomology
Serre duality, Riemann-Roch on curves
morphisms with connected fibres, Iitaka fibrations.
Prerequisites:
Linear Algebra I and II, Algebra, Commutative Algebra and Introduction to Algebraic Geometry (recommended).
Lectures:
Tuesdays 16-18 in SR10 (316), Gebäude E2.4
Wednesdays 10-12 in SR10 (316), Gebäude E2.4
Exam Criteria
Example Sheets will appear on this webpage each week, which have to be solved until the following week (exception: see the first Example Sheet).
The solutions should be uploaded via CMS
. The Sheets will be corrected and returned in the exercise sessions.
In order to obtain a grade, the following criteria are to be fulfilled: at least 50% of the points from the exercise sheets attained, and the passed exam.
Exam Dates
The exam will be an oral exam, the dates will be individually arranged.
Important:
If you cannot register in the LSF for the exam, please send an email to Dr. Alessandro (your Prüfungssekretariat has to send us a confirmation that you are allowed to take part in the exam
well in time
before the exam). If your Prüfungsordnung allows it, you may use the second try to improve the grade.
Exercise Sessions
During the exercise sessions, solutions to the exercise sheets are presented, and students have the opportunity to ask questions about the lecture material, the grading of the submitted exercise sheets, or any general issues related to the exercise sheets.
Exercise Class:
Wednesdays 14-16 in SR1 (U.37), Gebäude E2.5
Tutor: Laura Stokkermans
Office hours: Tuesdays 11-12 in Office 313 (Gebäude E2.4)
