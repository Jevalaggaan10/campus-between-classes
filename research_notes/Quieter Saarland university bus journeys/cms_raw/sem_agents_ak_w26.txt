Seminar - Who does the science in the age of agents?
How agentic AI alters the academic landscape.
The fast development of large language models (LLMs) in recent years is starting to drive scientific discovery. Large-language-model agents are evolving from interactive assistants to full scientific operators: they write code, inspect data, launch analyses, design new methods, and draft reports. In this seminar we explore how agentic AI is altering the academic landscape with a focus on bioinformatics/biomedical research and have a closer look at the historic innovations that sparked this development. We divide the seminar into four broad categories:
Basic concepts
Multi-agent labs
Lab in the loop systems
Risks and Limitations
Organization:
Wednesday 10:15-12:00, first presentation 04.11.2026
Kick-off: 21.10.2026, 10:00
Attendance is mandatory!
Formalities:
Presentations are 30 minutes, followed by a 30-minute discussion. Students will be evaluated on how well they present their paper(s), answer questions, and participate in the discussion rounds (including asking questions themselves).
This course is aimed at Master's students in Bioinformatics, Computer Science, and DSAI. Prior knowledge of Machine Learning or a related lecture is recommended.
Bachelor students may also register for the seminar (as a proseminar worth 5 CP), but only a limited number of slots are reserved for them.
Time Table:
After the kick-off meeting on the 21st October, the course will take place weekly from 4th of November onward. In each session we will listen and discuss the presentation of one student to guarantee enough time for Q&A sessions.
