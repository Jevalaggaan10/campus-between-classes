    Algebraic Geometry

     

    Lecturer: Prof. Dr. Vladimir Lazić
    Office hours: by appointment via email.

    Assistant: Dr. Massimiliano Alessandro
    Office hours: by appointment via email.

     

    The course will be held in English.

     

    If a lecture takes place online, then the recorded Video-Lecture will be uploaded to this webpage. Each video will be available online for two weeks and then deleted.

     

    Topics

    The content includes: 

    • sheaves and schemes
    • morphisms between sheaves and schemes
    • abstract varieties
    • properness and separatedness
    • projective morphisms, basepoint free divisors, ample divisors, blowups
    • sheaves of modules, invertible sheaves and divisors
    • differentials and canonical sheaves, smoothness revisited
    • cohomology of sheaves, Čech cohomology
    • Serre duality, Riemann-Roch on curves
    • morphisms with connected fibres, Iitaka fibrations.

    Prerequisites: Linear Algebra I and II, Algebra, Commutative Algebra and Introduction to Algebraic Geometry (recommended).

    Exam Criteria

    Example Sheets will appear on this webpage each week, which have to be solved until the following week (exception: see the first Example Sheet). The solutions should be uploaded via CMS. The Sheets will be corrected and returned in the exercise sessions.

    In order to obtain a grade, the following criteria are to be fulfilled: at least 50% of the points from the exercise sheets attained, and the passed exam.

     

    Exam Dates

    The exam will be an oral exam, the dates will be individually arranged. 

    Important: If you cannot register in the LSF for the exam, please send an email to Dr. Alessandro (your Prüfungssekretariat has to send us a confirmation that you are allowed to take part in the exam well in time before the exam). If your Prüfungsordnung allows it, you may use the second try to improve the grade.

     

    Exercise Sessions
