    Back to the Roots: Graph Algorithms for Small Molecules

    This seminar explores fundamental graph-based algorithms underlying modern cheminformatics, including molecular graph matching, ring perception, molecular descriptors, fingerprints, fragmentation, and conformer generation. Students will study selected seminal papers to understand the theoretical foundations and algorithmic concepts behind these methods and gain hands-on experience by implementing and applying them to molecular data. Through two presentations, students will first introduce their chosen method and later present and critically discuss their implementation, results, and practical insights. The seminar aims to bridge chemical graph theory, algorithmic thinking, and practical cheminformatics while developing skills in scientific reading, implementation, analysis, and communication

    Prerequisits:

    The course has no formal prerequisites for participation. However, participants should have prior knowledge from the following courses in order to participate successfully, as we assume familiarity with the relevant content and build upon it. Consequently, we will prioritize students, who successful participated in these courses:

    • Algorithms and Data Structures (core lecture)
    • Programming
    • Open-Source Software and Libraries for Data Driven Drug Design
