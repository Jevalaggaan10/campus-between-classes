    Zusätzlich zu den regulären Sprechstunden der Tutorinnen und Tutoren bietet Tobias Mai eine "erweiterte deutschsprachige Sprechstunde" an. Im Rahmen dieser Veranstaltung können einzelne Themen der Vorlesung wiederholt bzw. etwas detaillierter besprochen werden. Nähere Informationen finden Sie hier.

    Die "erweiterten deutschsprachigen Sprechstunden" finden während der Vorlesungszeit (ab dem 23. Oktober 2026) freitags um 14 Uhr (c.t.) im Hörsaal III in Gebäude E2 5 statt. Alle Termine stehen auch im Kalender.

    In addition to the regular office hours held by the tutors, Tobias Mai offers an "extended office hour"; by decision of the faculty, these office hours will be held exclusively in German. During these events, specific topics from the lecture can be reviewed or discussed in greater detail. Further information can be found here.

    The "extended German-language office hours" will take place during the lecture period (starting October 23, 2026) on Fridays at 2:15 pm in Lecture Hall III (Hörsaal III) in Building E2 5. All scheduled dates are also listed in the calendar.

    Mathematics for Computer Scientists I

    Lecturer: Prof. Dr. Peter Ochs

    Assistant: Dr. Tobias Mai

     

    General information

    • The lectures will be given by Prof. Dr. Peter Ochs.
    • The lecture course begins on Wednesday, October 14, at 10:15 am.
    • Online attendance is not possible, but we will provide recordings of the lectures.
    • The lectures are held exclusively in English. However, we offer tutorials in both English and German; you can indicate your language preference when registering for the tutorials. (Registration for the tutorials is possible in CMS during the first week of lectures. More information will follow later.)

     

    Content

    This lecture course is the first part of a three-semester course in mathematics for computer science students.

    In this first part, the emphasis is on basic notions of mathematics and the calculus of functions in one real variable.

     

    Lecture times

    • Wednesday, 10:15-11:45: Günter Hotz lecture theatre, Building E2 2
    • Friday, 10:15-11:45: Günter Hotz lecture theatre, Building E2 2

    You can find a map showing all the buildings on campus here.

     

    References
