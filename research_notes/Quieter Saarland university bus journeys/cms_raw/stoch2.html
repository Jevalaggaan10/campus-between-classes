    Stochastics II

    Henryk Zähle
    Registration for this course is open until Monday, 26.10.2026 23:59.

    News

    Currently, no news are available

    Contents

    This course follows on from Stochastics I and covers the following topics:
     
    • Conditioning on σ-algebras (conditional expectations, conditional distributions)
    • Basic concepts of stochastic processes
    • Kolmogorov’s extension theorem
    • Poisson processes
    • Gaussian processes
    • Brownian motion
    • Markov processes
    • Martingales

     

    Recommended prerequisites

    A solid background in mathematics and a good knowledge of measure-theoretic probability theory at the level of Stochastics I.
