Paper assignments
Hi all,
I have now emailed everyone who has been assigned a paper. I believe almost everyone received one of their top three choices.
If you have been assigned a paper but no longer wish to take the seminar,
please let me know as soon as possible
, so that we can offer your place to another student.
If you have not received such an email, I want to first thank you for your interest. At the same time, the places in the seminar are unfortunately limited and I’m sorry that we were unable to offer you a place this time around. You are still welcome to attend the seminar sessions to participate and discuss. However, you won't be able to receive credits for this seminar.
Tutorial: Friday, 30 October 2026
We will have a tutorial next Friday. Please
post your questions or suggested topics
on the
course forum
.
The tutorial is intended especially to support those presenting papers in the
Expressivity
and
Length Generalization
blocks. If you have been assigned a paper in either block, please
skim your paper beforehand
and use the forum to suggest topics or concepts you would like a crash course on. Your suggestions will help me tailor the tutorial to what you need.
See you all next week, and have a nice weekend!
Best,
Yash
