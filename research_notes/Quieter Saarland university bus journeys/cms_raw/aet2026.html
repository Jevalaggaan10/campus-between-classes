    Artificially Empathic Technology

    Artificially Empathic Technology (AET) extends the perspective of Affective Computing from recognizing, modeling, and synthesizing emotional behavior to the design of systems that can interpret human experience, support emotion or social regulation, and act sensitively in social interaction. Guided by the AET framework, the lecture explores how affect, cognition, bodily expression, social values, and interpersonal dynamics can be operationalized in computational systems without reducing artificial empathy to mere emotion recognition. A central focus is translating competing psychological theories of emotion, appraisal, attachment, regulation, and social interaction into computational models, comparing them, and critically evaluating them. In doing so, the lecture also introduces key insights from psychological methodology: how theoretical constructs are defined, measured, validated, and transformed into human-centered technologies for domains such as games, immersive environments, education, and social assistance. Master students will gain a strong background in the theory and practice of human-centered computing as it relates to games, immersive environments, and pedagogical applications.

    1 22.10.2026 CS, Psy From Social Computing to AET The differences between emotion-adaptive and artificially empathic technology, the AET definition, example domains such as training, education, health coaching and care, and the course and project formats. –
    2 29.10.2026 Psy Affect, Emotion, Mood and Other Related Concepts The affect trinity; basic, dimensional, appraisal and constructivist models; the component model and observable signals, and their limitations ("no fingerprint"). –
    3 05.11.2026 Psy Methods I: Thinking like an Experimenter Science vs. common sense, from observation to hypothesis, correlation vs. experiment, independent and dependent variables, between- and within-subject designs, threats to validity. Find teammates
    4 12.11.2026 Psy Empathy, Theory of Mind, Mentalization Cognitive vs. affective empathy, compassion, Theory of Mind (ToM), mentalization, CASA / Media Equation and the persona effect. Kickoff: teams, domain, scenario
    5 19.11.2026 CS, Psy Computational Affect + ALMA Explorer (hands-on) OCC appraisal theory, the OCEAN personality model, the PAD mood model and the decay of emotional intensity. Hands-on: modelling characters in ALMA Explorer. Research question + hypothesis
    6 26.11.2026 CS Interactive Systems + VisualSceneMaker web (hands-on) The agentic SIA research framework VisualSceneMaker: scenes and sceneflows, and an overview of the plugins (ALMA, LLM, ASR, TTS, Wizard of Oz). First prototype
    7 03.12.2026 Psy Methods II: Measurement and Running a Study Questionnaires and scale quality, relational measures (felt security, perceived responsiveness, empathic accuracy), power and sample size, pilot study, preregistration, research ethics (consent, data protection), and the VisualSceneMaker StudyMaster. –
    8 10.12.2026 Eth, Psy Values and Norms: What an Empathic System Has to Achieve Fundamental human values such as safety, autonomy and self-respect, in the context of goals, social roles and cultural norms; display rules.     9 17.12.2026 Psy, CS AET I: Regulation and the Individual Predictive processing, Moser & von Zeppelin (structural, situational and communicative emotions), self vs. display regulation, MARSSI. Prototype check

    11 14.01.2027 Psy, CS AET II: Relationship and Social Interaction Person objects and relationships; inner and outer communication paths. Regulation vs. co-regulation, especially repair, as shown in Mentalization-Based Therapy. Analysis
    12 21.01.2027 CS, Eth AET III: Making Values Computable, and Limits Cost function over the value goals, uncertainty, explainability, role and limits of LLMs, deception and dependency, uncanny valley, governance (EU AI Act, MEESTAR), outlook. Analysis
