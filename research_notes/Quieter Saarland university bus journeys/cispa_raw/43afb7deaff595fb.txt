Security
The Security core lecture ("Stammvorlesung") will be offered in the winter term 2026/2027.
Lectures
We plan to offer the lecture as a in-person class. Note: First lecture will be on October 14 with an Introduction. The main lecture content starts in the second week. In general, classes will be in-person.
The schedule for the two lectures per week is as follows:
Monday 12:15-13:45 (GHH, E 2.2)
Wednesday 12:15-13:45 (GHH, E2.2)
Registration
Registration in this CMS is required until October 31, 2026. LSF exam registration is required to participate in the exams (and will be possible until 1 week before the final exam). Please contact your Prüfungsamt for any problems with LSF registrations. Only for Erasmus students a registration solely on CMS is sufficient. Any other student cannot write the exam unless registered on LSF. We cannot register you on LSF. We will remove any CMS registrations for students that are not qualified (due to missing exercise points) or missing LSF registration.
