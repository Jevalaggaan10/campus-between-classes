# Broader Saarbrücken campus schedules

## How broadly were current semester schedules collected?

### Takeaway
The extraction downloaded 14 selected official LSF catalog branches successfully for Winter 2026/27, including all five main Saarbrücken faculty branches. It is substantially broader than the CS catalogs, but it is a catalog census of those selected branches, not a census of campus attendance.

### Cited Findings
- The current catalog uses `root120262` and displays **WiSe 2026/27**. The root distinguishes the five main Saarbrücken faculties, the Medical Faculty, and other offers. The Medical Faculty branch was deliberately not downloaded because most of its teaching is in Homburg. [Official current catalog root](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- The downloaded main faculty branches were **HW** (empirical human sciences and economics), **MI** (mathematics and computer science), **NT** (natural sciences and technology), **P** (humanities), and **R** (law). The extraction also covered international-student offers, language courses, certificates, Career Center, GradUS, entrepreneurship, teaching and learning, Transform4Europe, and prospective-student offers. [HW](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C458383&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [MI](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C469204&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [NT](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C468295&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [P](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C464258&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [R](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C458584&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total).
- **Computed extraction counts:** 1,991 unique LSF course IDs, 2,955 distinct schedule-pattern rows after cross-listing deduplication, and 1,360 included recurring weekday patterns across 1,038 course IDs. All 14 branch downloads succeeded. The denominator includes untimed, block, one-off, online, exam and unknown-location courses; it does not imply 953 missing weekly classes. These counts are our computation from the cited branch tables, not official enrollment figures. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- Metadata from every unique course is retained in `broader_source_inventory.csv`; every extracted timetable pattern, including excluded rows, is in `broader_meetings.csv`. Full branch URLs, request status and byte counts are in `broader_fetch_log.json`; raw HTML and reproducible scripts are retained locally. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).

### Inferences
- These sources materially reduce the risk of recommending a time that is quiet only in CS. They cannot identify the number of passengers traveling toward HTW/SWS, since a course can have a few or hundreds of attendees and different students have different destinations.
- The five faculty subtrees are the useful broad backbone. Degree-program branches were not separately crawled because they largely cross-list the same courses; omissions unique to those degree branches remain possible.

### Gaps
- The total number of unique courses across the entire university catalog was not established. Do not describe the 1,991 IDs as all university courses.
- There were 876 schedule patterns with no room and 70 with locations not conservatively classified as the main campus. These were excluded from the default model. The latter include Dudweiler buildings DUD5/DUD6, HN sports facilities, and some medical-style building numbers.
- The browser/web-fetch service timed out on broad LSF pages, but direct public HTTP requests succeeded. No authentication or access restriction was bypassed.

## What broad timing waves does the catalog show?

### Takeaway
The raw printed schedule patterns have strong boundaries around even hours and academic-quarter endings. Friday has fewer included patterns than the other weekdays, but this does not establish a passenger-count ratio.

### Cited Findings
- **Computed weekday pattern counts before merging CMS/CISPA duplicates:** Monday 270, Tuesday 338, Wednesday 311, Thursday 315, Friday 126. This counts included recurring course patterns, including explicitly labeled distinct groups, not students. Unlabelled simultaneous room/source patterns are counted once per course boundary by default. [HW](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C458383&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [MI](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C469204&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [NT](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C468295&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [P](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C464258&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total), [R](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C458584&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total).
- **Computed printed end-time concentrations across the whole teaching week:** 11:45 (153 patterns), 12:00 (210), 13:45 (104), 14:00 (147), 15:45 (107), 16:00 (183), 17:45 (54), and 18:00 (90). These are nominal/literal source times before academic-quarter sensitivity analysis, frequency weighting, date restrictions and CMS reconciliation. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- Introductory business administration has a Monday 10:00–14:00 recurring printed slot in the Auditorium Maximum starting 19 October. The initial 12 October 12:15–14:00 slot is recorded separately and excluded from the recurring model. [LSF course 167579](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=167579&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung).
- Mathematics for economists lists its Tuesday lecture 10:15–11:45 in the Auditorium Maximum and separate exercise slots, including Tuesday 14:15–15:45. Accounting lists Thursday 10:15–11:45 and 16:00–17:30 in the Auditorium Maximum. [Mathematics course 167251](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=167251&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung), [Accounting course 167372](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=167372&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung).
- Microeconomics has Tuesday and Wednesday 16:00–18:00 printed patterns in B4 1. Analysis I has Monday and Wednesday 10:00–12:00 in E2 5. Experimental Physics I has Monday and Tuesday 08:30–10:00 lecture patterns as well as separate exercise patterns. [Microeconomics 167137](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=167137&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung), [Analysis I 166958](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=166958&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung), [Experimental Physics I 166304](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?state=verpublish&status=init&vmfile=no&publishid=166304&moduleCall=webInfo&publishConfFile=webInfo&publishSubDir=veranstaltung).

### Inferences
- A useful heatmap should convolve these class boundaries with walking/boarding lead and lag times, then evaluate actual HTW/SWS bus journeys. A heatmap of occupied classrooms is a different quantity and would not answer the bus question directly.
- The large printed even-hour concentrations support the user's observation, but c.t. ambiguity means precise minute rankings require sensitivity analysis rather than assuming every 12:00 end is literal.

### Gaps
- LSF room reservations can include several simultaneous rooms for one course. In particular `lsf_165929` (Studienbegleitende Deutschkurse ISZ Saar) has many 16:30–20:45 room patterns without group labels. They may represent parallel language groups or broad bookings. Default inclusion now collapses unlabelled simultaneous room patterns to one representative course boundary; 95 extra room/source patterns are excluded from default counts across the entire dataset. Every raw room pattern remains auditable. Explicit source group IDs stay separate. The quantitative researcher was also warned to check a wider same-course boundary cap as a sensitivity scenario.
- A lecture ending does not mean every student leaves campus. The data does not link the same student's successive courses or home destination.

## What assumptions and exclusions must the heatmap preserve?

### Takeaway
Use `include_in_demand=1` as the default LSF input filter, retain source time conventions, and reconcile course IDs/titles with CMS/CISPA before aggregation. Model outputs should be described as scheduled demand proxies.

### Cited Findings
- Rows are included by default only when they have a Monday–Friday start/end time, a weekly or fortnightly recurrence, a teaching-period overlap, and a recognizable main-campus building (A–E) or CISPA/DFKI/MPI location. Online course types, unknown locations, exams/consultations, single dates mislabeled weekly and one-off/block events are excluded. These are extraction rules applied to the official timetable fields. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- `first_date`, `last_date`, `raw_frequency`, `raw_date_span` and `canceled_dates` preserve source restrictions. `meeting_unit_key` and `simultaneous_room_count` flag shared course boundaries; unverified additional simultaneous room patterns have `include_in_demand=0`. Source intervals of at least four hours are flagged as long booking blocks in `notes`; actual teaching boundaries are unverified. When no dates are printed, the extraction uses 12 October–5 February as broad bounds and marks that assumption in `notes`. `broader_dated_events.csv` separately retains block/one-off rows. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- `time_convention=unknown` marks printed full-hour intervals without explicit c.t./s.t.; `literal` marks intervals with specific non-full-hour minutes and does not assert explicit s.t.; `ct`/`st` are used only when the source text explicitly marks them. No quarter-hour shift was silently applied in this dataset. [Current catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step).
- `max_participants` is populated only where LSF prints a course capacity. Capacity is not attendance, and neither capacity nor number of timetable rows is a measured bus-passenger count. [HW catalog examples](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=lang&root120262=461590%7C458383&search=1&state=wtree&trex=total).

### Inferences
- Compare literal and c.t. timing scenarios for unknown full-hour slots; favor recommendations that remain quiet across both. Count once per physical course meeting while preserving genuinely distinct tutorial groups.
- CMS/chair corrections can be more precise than LSF nominal slots. Keep the exact/corrected source where the same course meeting appears in both; LSF adds broad non-CS coverage and otherwise missing groups.

### Gaps
- No live vehicle occupancy, actual attendance, route-choice proportions, bus capacity, passenger origin/destination or probability of getting a seat was obtained.
- Holiday boundaries and exceptional weeks need explicit handling. LSF recurrence tables can mechanically include dates during university breaks; they are not independent evidence that teaching happens during those breaks.
- Source fetches were all successful, but some source schedules are plainly incomplete, nominal or outdated. The final map must retain these limitations rather than describe a complete passenger census.
