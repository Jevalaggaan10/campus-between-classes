# Time your Saarland buses between classes

**A useful repeatable morning choice is the 101 from HTW/SWS at 09:02, reaching Universität Campus at 09:23.** It falls between the main class-arrival waves in this timing model on every weekday; Tuesday and Friday also have a good later option, the 124 at 10:22 → 10:43. Homeward choices vary more by day, as the table below shows. The analysis combines **1,471 meeting patterns across 1,062 canonical timed courses** for Winter 2026/27. It estimates pressure from published class schedules, rather than observed bus loads, so a low score is a departure worth trying—not a guarantee of a seat. Exact bus times come from the current operator tables, checked on 26 October 2026. ([101 timetable](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664471a35c), [124 timetable](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664516933a), [operator timetable index](https://www.saarbahn.de/fahrplan/fahrplaene))

## Try these weekday departures between class waves

These are low-pressure options within convenient presentation bands: campus arrival **08:00–11:00** and campus departure **15:00–19:30**. You specified no travel-hour constraint, so the heatmap and full sorter also cover **06:00–22:00**, including midday and early/late alternatives. Several trips tie; the table does not imply one uniquely optimal bus.

| Day | Toward university: HTW/SWS → Universität Campus | Homeward: Universität Campus → HTW/SWS |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | **101 09:02 → 09:23** | **112 15:10 → 15:31** |
| Tuesday | **124 10:22 → 10:43** | **124 16:50 → 17:11** |
| Wednesday | **101 09:02 → 09:23** | **124 17:20 → 17:41** |
| Thursday | **101 09:02 → 09:23** | **112 15:10 → 15:31** |
| Friday | **124 10:22 → 10:43** | **112 15:30 → 15:51** as an earlier practical choice; **124 18:50 → 19:11** has the lowest modeled daytime score |

The table’s clock times are verified in the [101 toward campus](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664471a35c), [124 toward campus](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664516933a), [112 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644d6cf50), and [124 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26645116974) tables. The 112 choices are lecture-day services. Friday’s earlier 112 has only about one meeting-equivalent of pressure in the higher timing scenario; waiting until 18:50 for a slightly lower index has no demonstrated passenger benefit. Another early Friday option is **109 Campus 15:29 → HTW/SWS 16:00**. ([109 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644b8fc21))

For consistency, **101 09:02 → 09:23 works as a morning option all five days**. A lecture-day 112 has the same departure and arrival times; their timing scores tie, and the model cannot establish which already carries fewer riders. On Monday and Thursday, **124 Campus 15:20 → HTW/SWS 15:41** is another useful return. On 112/109/124, terminal boarding is three minutes before the listed Campus time—for example, Monday’s 112 leaves Busterminal at **15:07**. That places you before the Campus pickup, although terminal queues remain possible. ([112 toward campus](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26644c9f73e), [124 toward town](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a26645116974))

## The heatmap explains the even-hour crowd waves

Across weekdays, the strongest modeled arrival waves cluster around **09:45–10:15, 11:45–12:15 and 13:45–14:15**. Homeward pressure commonly rises around **11:50–12:25, 13:50–14:25 and 15:50–16:25**, with weekday-specific evening differences. These are broad model windows, not precise forecasts for an individual bus. The published timetable contains many quarter-to and full-hour class endings; walking and waiting shift them into the even-hour bus crowds you observed. UdS’s own guide explains that a nominal 10–12 class commonly runs 10:15–11:45, while exact course notices take priority. ([UdS class-time guide](https://www.uni-saarland.de/fileadmin/upload/global/welcome-center/nadu.pdf), [computed timing vectors](<C:/codex/reports/saarland-class-time-vectors.csv>))

**Choose the campus arrival time for inbound travel and the campus departure time for homeward travel.** For example, a 124 leaving HTW/SWS at :52 arrives on campus at :13, close to common :15 class starts; the home departure minute alone is misleading. Neither :20 nor :50 is universally quieter homeward: the best phase depends on the day’s actual class boundaries. Friday has fewer collected meeting patterns—152 versus Tuesday’s 358 after merging—but this does not establish a corresponding passenger ratio. ([124 toward campus](https://www.saarbruecken.de/media/download-6a2664516933a), [merged class data](<C:/codex/reports/saarland-canonical-class-meetings.csv>))

## The vectors preserve timing and expose coverage gaps

The extraction audited **74 CMS listings**, **27 CISPA listings**, **50 seminars**, **9 proseminars**, and **1,991 unique LSF IDs across 14 branches**, including the five main Saarbrücken faculties. These catalogs overlap and their counts must not be added as unique courses. dCMS had no public Winter 2026/27 section, so older semesters were excluded. Of 3,210 normalized input patterns, **1,471 were retained, 1,656 excluded, and 83 merged**; the merges comprise 74 cross-source mirrors and nine shared physical slots. Unknown rooms, online teaching, exams, one-off activity, optional consultations and clear non-teaching room bookings are separated in the audit files. Unverified simultaneous room bookings were consolidated while explicit tutorial groups remained distinct. ([CMS](https://cms.sic.saarland/system/courses), [dCMS](https://dcms.cs.uni-saarland.de/system/courses), [CISPA](https://cms.cispa.saarland/system/courses), [seminars](https://seminars.cs.uni-saarland.de/wise2627seminars), [proseminars](https://seminars.cs.uni-saarland.de/wise2627proseminars), [current LSF catalog](https://www.lsf.uni-saarland.de/qisserver/rds?P.vx=kurz&root120262=461590&search=1&state=wtree&trex=step))

Each course has **two sparse 965-dimensional timing vectors**: five weekdays × 193 five-minute bins, one vector for advertised starts and one for ends. Entries retain expected weekly meeting frequency. This gives a direct timing embedding; semantic text similarity would not identify synchronized releases. The actionable profile averages the **13 remaining ordinary teaching weeks, 26 October–5 February**, excluding **21 December–1 January**. Dated meetings, fortnightly frequencies and documented cancellations modify their contributions. It does not reconstruct the semester’s earlier startup weeks. ([semester dates](https://www.uni-saarland.de/dezernat/ls/termine.html), [embedding export](<C:/codex/reports/saarland-course-time-embeddings.json>))

For each bus, the model spreads class-start pressure over campus arrivals from 35 minutes early to 10 minutes late, peaking 10 minutes before class. It spreads class-end pressure from five minutes before to 40 minutes after the end, peaking 10 minutes afterward. These buffers are planning assumptions. Two timing scenarios test nominal versus academic-quarter interpretations, preserving explicit course corrections; **683 patterns still have uncertain time convention**. The map shows their average and scenario range, and bus sorting uses the higher scenario, rounded to 0.1 for ties. The range is timing sensitivity, not a statistical confidence interval. Meetings have equal weight; no enrollment, room capacity or passenger counts were invented.

The full-day ranking contains many **zero or negligible-pressure ties**, including early/late edges and mid-period gaps. They are separate departures, not continuous quiet windows. A rounded zero can reflect a lack of collected class pressure or missing public schedules. Actual attendance, route choice, school passengers, workers and library/canteen activity remain unmeasured.

The bus extraction contains 165 distinct HTW/SWS timetable options, of which 160 fall inside the modeled hours. It excludes short runs that do not reach home: some morning 109 :29 trips end at Rathaus and many 112s end at Hauptbahnhof. The 101 return table omits an HTW/SWS minute, so none was fabricated. Current PDFs are valid from **1 July 2026**; future timetable changes and date-specific disruptions require the current journey planner. Treat the weekday choices as evidence-based trials, and adjust using what you observe on your actual route.
